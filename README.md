Project #1 Draw Something Cool in 3D!

For this first project, I made a plant! It wasn't inspired by any real-life plant; I just put procedural elements together until I got something that looked cool. And yes, it is all procedural and is controlled with a couple of variables.

The flower pot base is made using triangles and colored based on position. Then the dirt is a circle made of triangles with some translated triangles on top to add some depth. Then the stem of the plant is made with a triangle strip offset as it goes up and colored based on vertical position. Then the leaves around the outside are spread out using the golden ratio and are just made of simple triangles pointing down. Then, the complex part. Flowers are positioned and oriented based on the surface of the stem. Then, I spread petal locations around the normal vector. The petals themselves are made of triangles and are offset based on the orientation of the petal, with a small shift to add some depth.
