
**Height**:
**Range**: Very Short (0.1m-0.5m), Short (0.5m-1.5m), Medium (1.5m-3m), Tall (3m-8m), Very Tall (8m-20m).
**Growth Pattern**: Linear, Exponential, Randomized (affects growth progression).

**Stem Thickness**:
**Range**: Narrow (0.1x height), Moderate (0.5x height), Wide (1.0x height), Very Wide (1.5x height).


**Stalks**: Mutually exclusive with branches.
**Range**: 1, 2, 3, 4, 5




**Branch Density**: Determines how many branches appear per node.
**Range**: Sparse, Moderate, Dense, Very Dense.
**Distribution**: Uniform, Top-heavy, Random.

**Branch Angle**: Controls the angles of branches relative to the main stem.
**Range**: Upright (10°-30°), Moderate (30°-60°), Drooping (60°-90°).




**Leaf Density**: Controls the number of leaves per branch unit. If branches are present, this is the number of leaves per branch.
**Range**: Sparse, Moderate, Dense, Very Dense.

**Leaf Shape**: Shape of individual leaves.
**Types**: Oval, Round, Serrated, Needle, Lobed, Compound.

**Leaf Size**: Scale for individual leaves.
**Range**: Very Small, Small, Medium, Large, Very Large.
**Variance**: Consistent, Slightly Varied, Highly Varied.

**Leaf Arrangement**: Configuration of leaves around the stem.
**Types**: Alternate, Opposite, Whorled, Spiral.
**Distribution**: Uniform, Randomized.






**Flower Density**: How many flowers per branch unit. Mutually exclusive with flowers.
**Range**: Sparse, Moderate, Dense, Very Dense.

**Flower Size**: Controls the size of flowers.
**Range**: Tiny, Small, Medium, Large, Very Large.

**Flower Shape**: Overall form of flowers.
**Types**: Single Petal, Star, Cup, Trumpet, Bell, Composite.

**Flower Petals**: Number of petals on each flower.
**Range**: Single, Few (3-5), Moderate (6-8), Many (9+).

**Flower Color**: Color scheme of flowers.
**Options**: Warm (red/yellow/orange), Cool (blue/purple), Pastel, Vibrant, Exotic (multicolor/iridescent).
**Pattern**: Solid, Gradient, Spotted, Streaked.





**Fruit Density**: Number of fruits per branch unit.
**Range**: Sparse, Moderate, Dense.

**Fruit Shape**: Shape of individual fruits.
**Types**: Round, Oval, Elongated, Clustered, Star, Compound.

**Fruit Size**: Scale of fruits.
**Range**: Tiny, Small, Medium, Large, Very Large.
**Variance**: Consistent, Varied.

**Fruit Color**: Color scheme of fruits.
**Options**: Red Shades, Yellow/Orange Shades, Purple/Blue, Green Shades.
**Pattern**: Solid, Gradient, Speckled, Striped.






**Thorns**: Adds thorn density to branches or stems.
**Range**: None, Few, Moderate, Dense.

**Bark Texture**: Controls stem/branch texture.
**Options**: Smooth, Rough, Peeling, Ridged, Spiked.

**Growth Rate**: Speed at which plant grows.
**Range**: Slow, Moderate, Fast.
